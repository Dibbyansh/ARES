# 🚨 DISASTER MANAGEMENT TEAM

## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)

---

## 1. INCIDENT RESPONSE FRAMEWORK (ALL DISASTERS)

### Objective

Ensure rapid, coordinated, and safe response to minimize casualties and damage.

### Immediate Actions (First 5–10 Minutes)

* Activate Incident Command System (ICS)
* Assign Incident Commander (IC)
* Identify:

  * Type of incident
  * Location
  * Severity level
* Alert all relevant units:

  * Fire
  * Medical
  * Police
  * Utility teams

### Communication

* Establish primary communication channel (radio/control room)
* Share real-time updates across teams
* Maintain a single source of truth

### Area Control

* Secure perimeter
* Define zones:

  * Hot Zone (danger area)
  * Warm Zone (support operations)
  * Cold Zone (safe area)

---

## 2. FIRE INCIDENT RESPONSE

### Deployment

* Dispatch fire units immediately
* Assign teams:

  * Fire suppression
  * Search and rescue
  * Evacuation support

### Operations

* Identify fire type (electrical, chemical, structural)
* Contain fire spread
* Conduct systematic search for trapped individuals

### Support Actions

* Shut down electricity and gas supply
* Coordinate with facility management

### Medical Coordination

* Set up triage area
* Categorize victims:

  * Critical
  * Moderate
  * Minor

### Public Control

* Evacuate civilians in an orderly manner
* Restrict crowd access

---

## 3. FLOOD RESPONSE

### Deployment

* Deploy rescue boats and evacuation teams
* Position teams in high-risk zones

### Operations

* Identify flooded areas and stranded individuals
* Prioritize rescue:

  * Elderly
  * Children
  * Injured

### Utility Control

* Coordinate shutdown of electricity in affected areas

### Evacuation

* Relocate people to shelters or higher ground
* Ensure safe evacuation routes

### Health Measures

* Distribute clean water and essential supplies
* Monitor for disease outbreaks

---

## 4. ROAD ACCIDENT RESPONSE

### Deployment

* Dispatch ambulance and police immediately

### Scene Assessment

* Identify number of victims
* Assess injury severity
* Check for hazards (fuel leak, fire risk)

### Medical Actions

* Perform on-site triage
* Stabilize critical patients
* Transport to nearest hospital

### Traffic Control

* Secure accident site
* Divert traffic
* Prevent secondary accidents

### Documentation

* Record incident details
* Coordinate with law enforcement

---

## 5. EARTHQUAKE RESPONSE

### Deployment

* Activate search and rescue teams
* Deploy heavy equipment if required

### Operations

* Identify collapsed structures
* Conduct systematic rescue operations

### Risk Control

* Monitor aftershocks
* Mark unsafe structures

### Medical Setup

* Establish temporary medical camps
* Handle mass casualty cases

### Relief Support

* Set up shelters
* Provide food, water, and medical aid

---

## 6. ELECTRICAL INCIDENT RESPONSE

### Safety

* Ensure power supply is disconnected before intervention

### Coordination

* Work with electrical authorities

### Medical

* Treat electric shock victims
* Perform CPR if required

### Risk Prevention

* Check for fire hazards due to electrical faults

---

## 7. GAS LEAK RESPONSE

### Area Control

* Evacuate affected area immediately
* Establish safety perimeter

### Operations

* Identify source of leak
* Coordinate with gas authorities to stop leak

### Ventilation

* Ensure proper airflow

### Medical

* Treat inhalation cases
* Provide oxygen support if required

---

## 8. MEDICAL TRIAGE PROTOCOL

### Priority Levels

* Red (Critical): Immediate life-saving care
* Yellow (Serious): Urgent but stable
* Green (Minor): Minor injuries
* Black: Deceased or beyond recovery

### Actions

* Tag victims clearly
* Allocate resources based on priority

---

## 9. COMMUNICATION AND COORDINATION

### Internal

* Maintain constant communication between teams
* Use clear and simple updates

### External

* Provide verified information to public
* Avoid misinformation and panic

---

## 10. POST-INCIDENT ACTIONS

### Assessment

* Evaluate damage and response effectiveness

### Reporting

* Document:

  * Timeline
  * Actions taken
  * Challenges faced

### Improvement

* Identify gaps
* Update SOPs accordingly

---

## CORE PRINCIPLES

* Safety of responders first
* Coordination over individual action
* Controlled and structured response
* Clear communication at all times

---

## FINAL RULE

A well-coordinated team saves more lives than a fast but unorganized response.
